Privacy Policy

StellarForge is private.

Last updated: 2026-10-02 · Effective immediately

StellarForge ("the App", "we", "our") is a single-player constellation weaving atelier for iPhone. This policy explains what data the App handles. The short version: the App does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

1. Data we do not collect

StellarForge does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, account systems, or any networking code that exports user data. There is no server, no telemetry endpoint, and no third-party tracker of any kind. Your activity inside the App never leaves your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your iPhone, inside the App's sandbox:

  • Game progress — constellations forged, edges drawn, tier completion.
  • Statistics — streak count, fastest weave, total play time.
  • Achievements — milestones earned across the Weaver, Artisan, Scholar, and Curator categories.
  • Preferences — palette selection, sound and haptic toggles, custom background choice.
  • Custom backgrounds — photos you take with the camera or import from your library, used solely as forge backgrounds.

All of the above are stored using Apple's standard sandbox storage. They are never uploaded to a server and cannot be read by us or by anyone else.

3. Permissions

The App requests the following iOS permissions, and only when needed:

  • Camera — used to capture a custom forge background. The image is stored locally and never leaves your device. You may decline; the App remains fully usable.
  • Photo Library (read-only) — used to import an existing photo as a custom forge background. Images you do not select are not accessed.
  • Haptics — used for tactile feedback during weaving. Haptic feedback can be disabled in Settings.

StellarForge does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, microphone, health data, tracking, or any other sensitive iOS permission.

4. Apple frameworks used

The App uses only Apple's first-party SwiftUI, Foundation, AVFoundation (for sound playback), and Core Haptics frameworks. No third-party SDKs are bundled.

5. Children

StellarForge is suitable for all ages. It does not target children as a separate audience and does not collect any data from any user, minor or otherwise.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling StellarForge permanently deletes all locally stored data, including any custom backgrounds you captured. There is nothing to request from a server because nothing was ever sent to one.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and release the change together with an App Store update. We will not silently broaden data collection.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us at becipora79@gmail.com. We read every email.
