Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Open the Forge Room, look at the scattered ember-stars, and tap any two stars to weave a glowing line between them. Keep weaving until the lines match the hidden target shape. When it matches, the constellation locks in and is added to your Codex.

What are the four tiers?

Apprentice, Adept, Master, and Archmage. Each tier introduces more stars, longer target shapes, and tighter tolerances. The Archmage tier is reserved for the most intricate weaves in the atelier.

How does the Daily cipher work?

A new constellation is offered each day. Daily ciphers rotate by week, so every weaver faces the same shape on the same day. Return daily to build your streak and earn Scholar milestones.

Can I use my own photo as a background?

Yes. Open the Atelier tab and tap "Capture" to take a new photo, or "Import" to choose one from your library. The selected photo becomes the background of your Forge. Switch back to any of the four built-in palettes in Settings at any time.

What are the four palettes?

Lemon Gold, Twilight Violet, Emerald Lab, and Rose Quartz. Each one recolors the entire interface to a single mood. Pick the one that suits tonight's forge.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the App's sandbox. StellarForge has no account system, no analytics, and no network call. See the Privacy Policy for the full story.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Reset Profile. This will clear all constellations, statistics, achievements, and custom backgrounds from your device. The action cannot be undone, so please be certain.

The app crashed. What should I do?

Please email becipora79@gmail.com with: your iPhone model, iOS version, what you were doing when it crashed, and ideally a screenshot. We will investigate.

Does StellarForge support iPad or Mac?

StellarForge is currently iPhone only. We may explore other form factors in the future.

Still need help?
